## 1. What is a cookie?
A cookie is a small file stored on your device when you visit a website. We also use similar technologies (local storage).

## 3. Managing your consent
- On your first visit, a banner lets you **accept, reject or customize** non-essential cookies. Rejecting is as easy as accepting.
- No non-essential cookie is set before you agree, in line with the Congolese Digital Code and, where applicable, the GDPR.
- You can change or withdraw your choice at any time via the "Manage cookies" link in the footer. Your choice is kept for [12] months.
- You can also block cookies in your browser settings; some features may then stop working.

## 4. Third-party cookies
Third parties (e.g. LinkedIn, GitHub, YouTube) apply their own policies, for which the Club is not responsible.
